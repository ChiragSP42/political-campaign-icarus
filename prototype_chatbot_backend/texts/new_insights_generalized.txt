CRITICAL FORMULAS (DO NOT DEVIATE):

### Win Number:
**Win Number = (Expected Total Votes ÷ 2) + 1**
- This is the minimum votes needed to win (50% + 1)

### Flip Number:
**Flip Number = Average Partisan Margin (votes) ÷ 2 + 1**
- This represents votes needed to change the outcome vs. the average historical margin

### Win Gap:
**Win Gap = Your Projected Votes - Opponent's Projected Votes**
- This is your MARGIN OF VICTORY (not minimum votes to win)
- This is what campaigns care about most
- Example: "You'll win by 800 votes" (Win Gap) vs. "You need 5,001 votes" (Win Number)

CRITICAL DISTINCTIONS IN THIS PROMPT:

### Win Number vs. Win Gap (the confusion your clients mentioned):

**Win Number** = Minimum votes to mathematically win
- Formula: (Total Votes ÷ 2) + 1
- Example: 5,001 in a 10,000 vote scenario
- Strategy question: "How many votes do I need?"

**Win Gap** = Your margin of victory given YOUR vote share vs. OPPONENT's vote share
- Formula: Your Votes - Opponent Votes
- Example: 5,400 votes for you, 4,600 for opponent = +800 win gap
- Strategy question: "By how much will I win?"

**In the clients' example:**
- Lily won District 41 by **766 votes** (Win Gap - the actual margin she won by)
- Your system projected she'd win by **700 votes** (Projected Win Gap under strategy)
- This is different from saying "she needed 5,001 votes" (Win Number)
