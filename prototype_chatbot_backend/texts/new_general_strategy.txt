CRITICAL FORMULAS (DO NOT DEVIATE):

### Win Number Calculation:
**Win Number = (Expected Total Votes ÷ 2) + 1**

Example: If 10,000 expected votes in a scenario → Win Number = (10,000 ÷ 2) + 1 = 5,001 votes

### Flip Number Calculation:
**Flip Number = Average Partisan Margin (votes) ÷ 2 + 1**

Where "Average Partisan Margin" is calculated from partisan races (House of Delegates, State Senate, Governor, Presidential) in your district from past 5 years.

Example: If average partisan margin = 1,000 votes → Flip Number = (1,000 ÷ 2) + 1 = 501 votes

### Win Gap Calculation:
**Win Gap = Your Projected Votes - Opponent's Projected Votes**

The Win Gap is NOT the same as Win Number. It represents your MARGIN OF VICTORY given your vote share and the opponent's vote share under specific turnout scenarios.

Example: 
- In a scenario with 10,000 votes
- You receive 5,400 votes
- Opponent receives 4,600 votes
- Win Gap = 5,400 - 4,600 = **+800 votes** (you win by 800 votes)

MANDATORY DATA COLLECTION & CALCULATIONS:

### Step 1: Identify All Precincts
- Extract all precincts in {district_name} for {current_year}
- Document precinct names exactly as they appear in official records
- Record registered voters per precinct

### Step 2: Historical Data Compilation
For EVERY precinct in {district_name}, retrieve:

**Partisan Races Only** (for margin calculations):
- House of Delegates results (all years)
- all other races and for all years that you think are important

For each race, record:
- Your party's vote total
- Opposition party's vote total
- Total votes cast
- Voter turnout (total votes / registered voters)

**All Races** (for turnout calculations):
- Include nonpartisan races and local races
- Used for overall turnout trend analysis

### Step 3: Calculate Average Partisan Margin
1. For each partisan race in your district (across all years), calculate: 
   **Margin = (Party A votes - Party B votes) ÷ Total votes cast**

2. Average ALL partisan race margins across the period:
   **Average Partisan Margin = Sum of all margins ÷ Number of partisan races**

3. Convert to vote margin: 
   **Average Vote Margin = Average Partisan Margin % × Current registered voters**

### Step 4: Calculate Historical Turnout Rates

1. Identify patterns:
   - **Races that could influence the candidates election**
   
2. Determine three turnout scenarios based on historical patterns:
   - **Base Turnout**: Conservative estimate
   - **Realistic Turnout**: Most likely scenario based on election type and recent trend
   - **High Turnout**: Optimistic scenario

### Step 5: Project Votes for Each Scenario
For your party in {district_name}:

1. Calculate historical vote share: 
   **Historical Vote Share = Average of your party votes ÷ Total votes (past 5 years from {current_year})**

2. For each turnout scenario:
   - **Expected Total Votes = Registered voters × Turnout Rate**
   - **Your Projected Votes = Expected Total Votes × Historical Vote Share**
   - **Opponent Projected Votes = Expected Total Votes - Your Projected Votes**
