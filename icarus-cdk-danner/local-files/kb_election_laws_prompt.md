ELECTION LAW KNOWLEDGE BASE RETRIEVAL PROMPT
============================================

OBJECTIVE:
Extract and organize election laws relevant to political campaign strategy, voter targeting, fundraising, advertising, and compliance requirements. This retrieval will populate structured placeholders in campaign analysis documents.

RETRIEVAL CONTEXT:
You are assisting a political campaign strategist who needs specific, applicable election laws to inform strategic decisions. Extract laws that directly impact campaign operations, not just general civics information.

---

QUERY FRAMEWORK:

When retrieving election laws, search for and extract information organized by these categories:

**1. VOTER PARTICIPATION & REGISTRATION**
- Voter registration deadlines and processes
- Registration eligibility requirements
- Voter ID/verification requirements
- Early voting rules and periods
- Mail-in/absentee voting procedures
- Same-day registration availability
- Voter purge/list maintenance schedules

**2. CAMPAIGN FINANCE & FUNDRAISING**
- Individual contribution limits (per candidate/election)
- PAC contribution limits and rules
- Donor disclosure thresholds
- Fundraising reporting deadlines and frequency
- Small-dollar fundraising exemptions (if any)
- In-kind contribution rules
- Contribution prohibition categories (corporate, foreign, etc.)

**3. ADVERTISING & VOTER OUTREACH**
- Disclaimer requirements for ads (digital, print, TV, radio)
- Prohibited advertising content or methods
- Advertising approval/filing requirements
- Robocall and phone contact restrictions
- Door-to-door canvassing rules and restrictions
- Text message/mobile communication regulations
- Social media advertising rules

**4. CAMPAIGN OPERATIONS & STAFFING**
- Permitted campaign worker activities
- Restrictions on government employee campaigning
- Conflict of interest rules
- Volunteer vs. paid staff classifications
- Record-keeping and documentation requirements
- Campaign committee registration requirements

**5. BALLOT ACCESS & CANDIDACY**
- Candidate filing deadlines and procedures
- Signature requirements for ballot access
- Petitioning procedures
- Filing fees
- Withdrawal procedures and deadlines
- Incumbent rules (if applicable)

**6. POLLING & SURVEY REQUIREMENTS**
- Poll disclosure requirements
- Polling methodology regulations
- Poll weighting/sampling rules
- Timeline restrictions on public polling

**7. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES**
- Polling location rules
- Poll observer/challenger rights
- Provisional ballot procedures
- Accessibility requirements
- Vote counting and certification procedures

**8. POST-ELECTION COMPLIANCE**
- Campaign expense reporting deadlines
- Final disclosure requirements
- Audit triggers and procedures
- Penalty/enforcement procedures

---

EXTRACTION REQUIREMENTS:

For each law retrieved:

**Applicable Jurisdiction:** [State/Local/Federal]

**Law/Regulation Name:** [Official title]

**Effective Date:** [Date]

**Key Provision Summary:** [Clear, concise explanation]

**Compliance Impact on Campaign Strategy:** [How this law affects campaign decisions]

**Deadline/Timing (if applicable):** [Specific dates/windows]

**Penalties for Non-Compliance:** [Consequences if violated]

**Source Citation:** [Where law is found - statute code, regulation code, etc.]

---

SEARCH STRATEGY:

Perform searches using these focused queries:

1. Search for specific **regulation categories** (e.g., "voter registration deadlines," "campaign finance limits")
2. Search for **compliance timing** (e.g., "disclosure deadlines," "report filing requirements")
3. Search for **forbidden activities** (e.g., "voter suppression prohibited," "prohibited campaign activities")
4. Search for **geographic specificity** (e.g., state-level vs. local ordinances that may differ)
5. Search for **recent changes** (e.g., "recent amendments to election law")

Organize all retrieved laws into this structured format:

---

QUALITY STANDARDS:

✅ Laws retrieved must be CURRENT (not outdated/repealed)
✅ Laws must be APPLICABLE to the specific election/jurisdiction
✅ Laws must directly impact CAMPAIGN STRATEGY (not general civics)
✅ Laws must include SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS (not vague summaries)
✅ All laws must include OFFICIAL CITATIONS
✅ Contradictions between state/local laws must be FLAGGED
✅ Recent amendments must be HIGHLIGHTED with dates

---

COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID:

❌ Retrieving general civics facts (not applicable to campaigns)
❌ Mixing federal, state, and local laws without clear distinction
❌ Omitting deadlines and timing requirements
❌ Providing outdated laws (verify current status)
❌ Missing penalties and enforcement consequences
❌ Failing to cite official sources
✅ Focus on ACTIONABLE requirements specific to campaign operations
