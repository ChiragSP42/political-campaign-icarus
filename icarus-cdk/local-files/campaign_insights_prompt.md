CRITICAL FORMULAS (DO NOT DEVIATE):

### Win Number:
**Win Number = (Expected Total Votes ÷ 2) + 1**
- Minimum votes needed to mathematically win

### Win Gap:
**Win Gap = Your Projected Votes - Opponent's Projected Votes**
- Margin of victory (positive = win, negative = loss)
- This is the strategic metric that matters for campaign planning
